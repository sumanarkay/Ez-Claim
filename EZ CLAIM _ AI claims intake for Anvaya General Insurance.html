AI Transformation Plan: insurance capstone

EZ CLAIM gets every motor claim decision-ready.

EZ CLAIM reads the documents on each motor claim, finds the policy clause that applies, flags anything that looks risky, and sends the file to the right path by repair value. It never rejects a claim and never sets the final amount: a handler or surveyor always decides.

Use case

Handlers spend their time re-checking papers, not deciding claims.

Today estimates are checked by hand, surveyors have backlogs, and customers are asked again for documents Anvaya already holds. EZ CLAIM removes the re-typing, the re-asking and the first-pass checking, so each file reaches a person ready to decide. The decision stays with the handler or surveyor every time.

Data science

Score the risk

Flags possible fraud with reasons a person can check. It never rejects a claim.

Rules

Route by value

Claims under ₹50,000 go on the fast path to a handler. Claims of ₹50,000 or more go to a licensed surveyor.

System prompt
You read a motor garage estimate.
Extract: registration, parts total, labour total.
Give a confidence from 0 to 1 for each field.
Never state a value that is not visible.
Do not judge coverage or fraud. Do not decide the claim.
Context
Document: garage estimate (estimate_PNQ_2231.pdf)
Fields requested: registration, parts total, labour total
Policy clause found by Knowledge: "Own damage: front bumper and headlamp covered. ₹1,000 compulsory deductible applies."

validate_fields() result:

Operating model and roadmap

Who runs it, under which rules, and the first 90 days.

Org structure

Hub and spoke

Anvaya builds the AI platform, governance and shared services once in a central hub, and Claims is the first spoke. Claims keeps ownership of results, and handlers and surveyors decide every claim. We would move towards a federated model if other business lines need their own AI teams or the hub becomes a bottleneck.

Strategy and portfolioClaims first; surveyor path, agent portal and flood damage wait on purpose.
Governance and riskMonthly AI council chaired by the COO, risk tiers, AI inventory.
People and rolesNamed owners, trained Pune handlers, champions on the floor.
Delivery and platformThree gates, an evaluation set, a tested rollback and one AI gateway.
Run and improveWeekly failure review; every incident becomes a new test.
Adoption and valueA person decides every claim; cycle time, re-requests and override rate tracked.
Days 1 to 30: shadow

Watch, change nothing

  • AI policy, COO-chaired council, risk tiers, AI inventory, DPDP data map.
  • Owners named; Pune's 18 handlers trained.
  • Thin slice runs in shadow: portal, router, document AI, audit store, gateway.
  • Baseline measured; evaluation set v1 built.
Gate 1, day 30Evaluation pass rate met, DPO and CISO sign off, deadline monitors live, rollback tested. The COO decides.
Days 31 to 60: canary

Small trial in Pune

  • Weekly failure review; incidents added to the evaluation set.
  • Champions on the floor; handlers decide every claim.
  • Policy search, access filter and workbench; 5% of Pune fast-path claims.
  • Weekly leading metrics; override rate watched.
Gate 2, day 60Override rate stable for two weeks, no AI-caused deadline breaches, incidents added to evaluations. The COO decides.
Days 61 to 90: gradual

Wider rollout

  • First fairness review; failover drill completed.
  • Train two more hubs; Pune handlers keep deciding every claim.
  • Go from 25% to 50%; fraud score advisory; photo checks in shadow.
  • Measure results against the baseline: cycle time, re-requests, override rate.
Gate 3, day 90Value shown against baseline and readiness questions answered. The Board decides tranche 2.
